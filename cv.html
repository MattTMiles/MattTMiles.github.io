Research interests

  • Improving pulsar timing and pulsar timing arrays, including novel methods such as profile-domain and simultaneous polarisation timing.
  • The nature of the stochastic gravitational-wave background.
  • Black holes, neutron stars and pulsars and their intrinsic properties, e.g. constraints on the nuclear equation of state and tests of gravity.
  • Modelling noise sources, both common and uncommon to sources in the gravitational-wave frequency band.
  • The impact of model misspecification.
  • Single sources of gravitational waves.
  • Population modelling of supermassive black-hole binaries.
