About

I'm a postdoctoral research fellow at Vanderbilt University.

I mainly focus on pulsars, pulsar timing arrays, and gravitational waves. I'm interested in how pulsars work, how we can use them better, and what information we can get out of the spacetime they measure changing.

Ambitious projects excite me, so I've ended up leading a lot on behalf of large collaborations - but I still make time to do a lot of fun research, too.
