I mainly work with millisecond pulsars, nature's best clocks, to see what makes them tick and to take a look at stretching spacetime, measuring the imprint of inspiraling supermassive black hole binaries.

I want to: time pulsars more precisely, understand why they do what they do, and measure gravitational waves with them. To help out I've spent time writing software that lets people do this, and I look into next-generation telescopes to predict what happens next.
